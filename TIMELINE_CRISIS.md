## PROBLEM 2: Danny's Game Boy Origin Story

### Game Gear Timeline Issue

**Chapter 1, Easter 1991:**
- Danny "had just turned nine"
- Has "a Game Boy he'd gotten for Christmas"
- Which Christmas? 1990? 1989?

**Chapter 2, December 1991:**
- Danny is 9 years old
- Line 27: "**Jake gave it to me when he got his Game Gear**"
- Line 27: "Since I was seven" = 2 years ago

**The Problem:**
- If Danny is 9 in Dec 1991, he was born ~1982
- He was 7 in 1989
- Game Boy DMG-01 (North America): **April 1989**
- Game Gear (North America): **April 1991**

**Contradiction:**
- Danny says he got it "when Jake got his Game Gear" (April 1991)
- But also "since I was seven" (1989)
- These can't both be true!

**Easter 1991 problem:**
- If Jake got the Game Gear in April 1991 (Game Gear launch)
- Then Danny got the Game Boy in April 1991
- But Easter 1991 is in late March/early April
- So Danny **just got** the Game Boy at Easter?
- But Chapter 1 says he got it "for Christmas"!
